1. What this covers

This policy explains the cookies and similar browser storage that [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] uses on the EcomFans application and marketing site. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy.

2. The short version

We use only strictly necessary cookies. We run no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party trackers on our own site. Under the EU ePrivacy Directive (and Portugal's Lei n.º 41/2004) cookies that are strictly necessary to deliver a service you asked for do not require consent, which is why you are not shown a cookie banner. We still have to tell you they exist, and this page does that.

5. Third-party cookies

We embed no third-party trackers. Two third parties are nonetheless involved in delivering the site:

  • Google Fonts — typefaces are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Google does not set cookies for font requests, but it does receive your IP address as part of the request. If you would rather it did not, we can self-host the fonts on request.
  • Stripe — when you start a checkout you are taken to Stripe's own hosted page. Stripe sets its own cookies there, including cookies used for fraud prevention, and those are governed by Stripe's cookie policy. We do not load Stripe scripts on our pages.

6. Pages published by our customers

EcomFans customers can publish their own landing pages through the funnel builder. Those pages are authored by the customer, and any cookie, pixel or tracking script on them is the customer's responsibility, not ours — the customer is the data controller for their own visitors. If you have arrived on a published page and want to know what it collects, contact whoever operates that page.

7. Managing cookies

Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies from its settings. Because our only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in, blocking it will stop you from logging in to EcomFans at all. Clearing it simply logs you out.

You can clear the browser storage listed in section 4 through your browser's "clear site data" tool; the only effect is that your theme choice and any unsaved editor drafts are forgotten.

8. Changes

If we ever introduce a cookie that is not strictly necessary, we will update this page and ask for your consent before setting it. The "last updated" date above always reflects the current version.
