2. The data we collect

  • Account data: your username, email address and a cryptographic hash of your password. We never store your password itself.
  • Content you upload: product photos, reference images, ad creatives, video and audio files, competitor ads, notes, scripts and the text prompts you write.
  • Content we generate for you: images, transcripts, analyses and landing pages produced from your inputs.
  • Billing data: your plan, credit balance, credit ledger entries and the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers. Card details are entered on Stripe's systems and never reach ours.
  • Advertising metrics you import: the contents of any Meta Ads export you upload, which contain your own campaign performance figures.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, request paths and timestamps used for rate limiting and abuse detection, failed-login counters and account lockout timestamps.
  • Error diagnostics: stack traces and request context when something breaks, sent to Sentry with passwords filtered out and personal-data collection disabled.
  • Optional AI provider keys: if you choose to supply your own Google or OpenAI API key, we store it encrypted (see section 7).

We do not knowingly collect special category data (Article 9 GDPR) and ask that you do not upload it. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data: for as long as your account exists, then deleted within 30 days of account closure.
  • Uploaded and generated content: until you delete it, or within 30 days of account closure.
  • Billing records: retained for [7] years after the transaction, because tax law requires it.
  • Security logs and rate-limit records: up to 90 days.
  • Error diagnostics: up to 90 days, per our Sentry retention setting.
  • Password reset tokens: expire after a few hours and are cleared once used.

7. How we protect it

  • All traffic is served over TLS, with HTTP Strict Transport Security enabled.
  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt (Werkzeug's default parameters). We cannot read them.
  • Any AI provider key you supply is encrypted at rest with Fernet — AES-128 in CBC mode, authenticated with HMAC-SHA256 — under a key derived from our application secret using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256.
  • Session cookies are HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite-scoped. Changing your password invalidates every other session.
  • Every request that changes data carries a CSRF token, and sensitive endpoints are rate limited.
  • Accounts lock temporarily after repeated failed logins.
  • Your files are stored under a per-account path and every media request is checked against the requesting account.

No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong, unique password, and tell us at once if you think your account has been accessed by someone else.
